About The Project

This project leverages AI and NLP to analyze research papers, generate summaries, extract important sections, and allow users to ask questions about their uploaded research papers.

Tech Stack: Python, Flask, AI/ML, NLP, PDF Parsing, LangChain, FAISS, Hugging Face and Groq.

Key Features

PDF Analysis

Upload research papers and extract their text and important sections automatically.

Summary Generation

Generate detailed AI-powered summaries for individual sections of research papers.

Intelligent Search

Ask questions about the uploaded research paper using retrieval-augmented generation.
